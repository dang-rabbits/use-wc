use-layout is a configurable flex-container primitive — like use-shell/use-card, it's styled by tag name but isn't a registered custom element, and needs the design-system stylesheet loaded. Attributes drive the flex container: direction (row default, or column), align, justify, gap (a named scale, xsmall…super), wrap, and inline.

By default use-layout doesn't touch its children. Give one direct child the fill attribute and it borrows use-shell's structure: the fill child grows to consume the remaining space and every other child is pinned to a fixed size. use-layout takes fill itself, so a nested growing (and scrolling) region needs no separate tag.
